NSDI 27
    Providence, Ri
    5/11-13/27
    Deadline 9/10/26
    Conference Focus: Design, implementation, and practical evaluation of networked and distributed systems. Seeks significant advances in networked services, distributed storage, cloud systems, networking infrastructure, and related systems topics. Emphasizes architectural novelty backed by convincing implementation and evaluation, with a particular interest in work that advances the state of networked systems. A new Frontiers Track also exists for bold, early-stage ideas that may not yet have exhaustive evaluation.
        Notes for us: Strong fit if vnIPFS is framed primarily as a decentralized *networked* storage system. The Frontiers Track is worth investigating. A main-track submission would need substantial scalability evaluation, realistic deployment experiments, comparisons against IPFS/Swarm, and a compelling demonstration that the proposed architecture improves real-world decentralized networking.
